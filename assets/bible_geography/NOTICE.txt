Offline Bible geography bundled from BiAY.

Bible place data: OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data, CC BY 4.0.
https://github.com/openbibleinfo/Bible-Geocoding-Data
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source revision and upstream transformations: manifest.json.

Land, rivers, lakes and context labels: Natural Earth, public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
Detailed physical sources and coverage: physical_sources.json.

ChurchProjection packaging changes: gzip JSON assets expanded into JSON and
embedded as Qt resources, including all 66 canonical book-to-place indexes.
BiAY offline-map styling and label hierarchy adapted for Qt. Display uses
Mercator projection, search, coordinate
entry, label collision avoidance, and selected-place highlighting. No network
map service is required. Artwork from the separate BiAY biblical_atlas pack
is not included in this coordinate-map bundle.

Ancient place identifications can be uncertain. Low-confidence labels carry a
question mark. Geographic features provide modern context, not reconstructed
ancient coastlines, political boundaries or water levels. No endorsement by
the data providers is implied.
